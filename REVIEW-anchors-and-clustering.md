# Anchors & clustering — changes for review

**Rules applied**

- **Anchors** (Y in the planning sheet) are must-dos, locked to their day.
- **Everything else is an option.** Each option sits on the day whose anchors it's closest to (within 2 km).
- **One day per place.** A place that showed up on two or more days now lives on one day only.

In the schedule, anchors show a **★ Anchor** badge. On the map they get a ring and a star.
Your flow on the day: go to the anchor, then use the Map tab's **Near me** to see every pin within 1.5 km, from any day.

## 6. How to keep this in sync

- **Make something an anchor:** add `anchor` to the card's class in `index.html`, e.g. `<div class="bl ts anchor">`.
  - Places in the card's 📍 line, title and option names become anchor pins.
  - Links inside the card's notes stay ordinary pins.
- **Mention a place without pinning it on that day:** add `data-nomap` to the link.
- **Move a place to another day:** move its card (or link) to that day's section. The map follows.
