# Should these become anchors?

An **anchor** is a must-do, locked to its day. On the Map tab, every other place that day is grouped under its nearest anchor, within 2 km.
So making something an anchor does two things:

1. It commits you to it.
2. It turns it into a "base" that nearby options gather around.

Below, each candidate gets the case for, the case against, and what changes on the map. Distances are straight-line.

---

## 1. Dohány Street Synagogue — Day 8 (Thu 5 Nov)

**Case for**

- It's Day 8's main daytime sight. Right now the day's only anchor is Szimpla Kert, at night.
- It's a timed, paid visit (~EUR 20pp, museum included). It takes a real block of time (~2h in your sheet), which is easy to lose if it's "optional".
- **Closed Saturdays.** Day 8 (Thursday) is your only realistic Budapest weekday for it if Day 7 stays full.

**Case against**

- It's 240 m from Szimpla Kert, so the Jewish Quarter options already group under Szimpla on the map.
- Your sheet didn't mark it, so you may be happy to just see the façade.

**On the map if anchored**

- Day 8 gets a second ★ base, in the morning.
- The Jewish Quarter options split between Dohány and Szimpla, by whichever is nearer:
  - Ludovika (280 m), Komondors (360 m) and Retrock (460 m) go to Dohány.
  - New York Café (800 m) also goes to Dohány.

**Recommendation:** ✅ **Anchor it.** It's the one Day 8 thing with fixed hours and a day restriction.

## 2. Heroes' Square + City Park — Day 8

**Case for**

- It's the far end of the Andrássy út walk, so it gives the middle of Day 8 a destination.
- It's the only base for the City Park cluster:
  - Vajdahunyad Castle, 390 m away
  - Széchenyi Thermal Baths, 530 m away, moved here from Day 7
  - These are 2.3 km from Szimpla, so today they sit under **"Other options"** with no anchor.
- Anchoring it would give that cluster a proper group and a dashed 2 km circle on the map.

**Case against**

- It's free and outdoors with no booking, so it's naturally flexible.
- It's 2 km out of the centre. If Day 8 runs late, it's the easiest thing to drop.

**On the map if anchored**

- A "Heroes' Square" group appears containing Vajdahunyad and the Baths.
- The Day 8 list reads as three blocks: Dohány (if anchored) / Heroes' Square / Szimpla.

**Recommendation:** ✅ **Anchor it if you intend to do the Baths.** That's what makes the trip out worth it. Otherwise leave it optional.

## 3. Kunsthistorisches Museum (KHM) — Day 9 (Fri 6 Nov)

**Case for**

- It was deliberately scheduled for Friday:
  - It's **closed Mondays**, so Day 5 is out.
  - It's **open until 21:00 on Fridays**.
- If it slips, there's no other day for it.
- It's a ~2h ticketed visit (EUR 21pp), and the main thing to do in Vienna after the train gets back.
- Café im KHM sits inside it (20 m), so it would group under it naturally.

**Case against**

- Day 9 is already a travel day: checkout, a 3-hour train, and the hotel.
- Your sheet left it unmarked, maybe on purpose, in case you're tired.
- It's 300 m from the Hofburg, so it's the same area as Day 1. But it isn't "the same place twice", and moving it to Day 1 would undo the Friday-late-opening logic.

**On the map if anchored**

- Day 9 Vienna gets a KHM group containing its café.
- Pöschl (950 m) and the Christmas market at Stephansplatz (1 km) stay under the final-dinner anchor.

**Recommendation:** ✅ **Anchor it.** It's the only day it can happen.

## 4. Hohensalzburg Fortress — Day 5 morning (Mon 2 Nov)

**Case for**

- It was an **anchor in your sheet's Option B**. With Werfen as Plan A, it's now on Day 5 morning with the rest of the Old Town.
- It's Salzburg's signature sight. If it stays optional, a slow morning before the 12:07 train could easily lose it.
- It would be the base for the Old Town cluster:
  - Residenzplatz, 390 m away
  - Mozart's Birthplace, 640 m away
  - Getreidegasse, 690 m away
  - Café Mozart, 680 m away
  - These currently sit under "Other options" on Day 5.

**Case against**

- Day 5 morning is tight: the funicular plus ~1.5h at the fortress, then back to Salzburg Hbf (2 km) for the **12:07** train with bags.
- If Plan B happens (rain on Day 3), you'll have done it on Day 3 already. The anchor would then be on the wrong day until it's moved.

**On the map if anchored**

- Day 5 shows a "Hohensalzburg Fortress" group with the whole Old Town under it.
- Hellbrunn and Untersberg (3.8 km+) stay under "Other options" as alternatives.

**Recommendation:** ⚖️ **Your call.**
- Anchor it if seeing the fortress matters more than a relaxed last Salzburg morning.
- Leave it optional if you'd rather keep the train stress-free.
